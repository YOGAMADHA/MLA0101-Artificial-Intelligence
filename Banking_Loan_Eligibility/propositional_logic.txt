PROPOSITIONAL LOGIC REPRESENTATION

Let:
I = Income requirement is satisfied
C = Credit score requirement is satisfied
E = Applicant is employed
L = Applicant has no existing loan
A = Applicant is eligible

Rule 1:
(I AND C AND E AND L) -> A

For the second rule:
I2 = Income is at least 50000
C2 = Credit score is at least 650

(I2 AND C2 AND E AND L) -> A

If neither rule is satisfied:
NOT A

Propositional logic represents each condition as a true/false proposition.
