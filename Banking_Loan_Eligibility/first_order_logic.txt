FIRST-ORDER LOGIC REPRESENTATION

Predicates:
Income(x, n)
CreditScore(x, s)
Employed(x)
NoExistingLoan(x)
Eligible(x)

Rules:

1. For every applicant x:
   (Income(x, n) AND n >= 30000 AND
    CreditScore(x, s) AND s >= 700 AND
    Employed(x) AND NoExistingLoan(x))
   -> Eligible(x)

2. For every applicant x:
   (Income(x, n) AND n >= 50000 AND
    CreditScore(x, s) AND s >= 650 AND
    Employed(x) AND NoExistingLoan(x))
   -> Eligible(x)

3. If no eligibility rule can be inferred for x:
   -> NotEligible(x)

First-order logic allows variables such as x, n and s and represents relationships
between an applicant and their attributes.
