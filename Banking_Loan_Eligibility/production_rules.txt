BANKING LOAN ELIGIBILITY - PRODUCTION RULES

Rule 1:
IF income >= 30000 AND credit_score >= 700
AND employment = employed AND existing_loan = no
THEN applicant is eligible.

Rule 2:
IF income >= 50000 AND credit_score >= 650
AND employment = employed AND existing_loan = no
THEN applicant is eligible.

Rule 3:
IF the applicant does not satisfy any eligibility rule
THEN applicant is not eligible.

Facts considered:
- Monthly income
- Credit score
- Employment status
- Existing loan status
